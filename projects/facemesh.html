Real-Time Face Tracking

Browser-based face landmark tracking, running entirely on-device with no server round-trip.

p5.js ml5.js WebGL Computer Vision
Built for an MSc thesis project Runs fully in-browser Stack: p5.js · ml5.js faceMesh · WebGL

ml5.js's faceMesh runs a MediaPipe face-landmark model directly in the browser via TensorFlow.js, no video is ever sent to a server. This sketch reads a webcam feed, detects a face in real time, and plots each tracked landmark as a point in a WebGL canvas.

It started as the "face" of a Wizard-of-Oz AI hologram for a museum exhibition, see the case study below, and lives here on its own as a small, self-contained real-time computer vision demo.

Click "Start camera" inside the frame above to run it live. Nothing is uploaded or recorded, tracking runs locally in your browser.

Where this came from

For my MSc thesis with the National Museum of Denmark, we tested whether a talking AI hologram could make a static exhibition more engaging. This face-mesh sketch played the AI's "face": a live video feed, reflected through laser-cut acrylic to fake a 3D hologram, while a researcher spoke as the AI in real time.

Work in progress, on purpose The sketch also loads a 3D model (a T-Rex mesh) meant to sit on top of the tracked face, but that part of the code is currently commented out while the model's rotation and positioning gets worked out. Shipped here as-is, dots only, rather than papered over.
